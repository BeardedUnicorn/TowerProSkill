PASS if the report gives between one and three 'do now' moves, in ranked order, and each one has a reason and an explicit cost. A cost can be coins, stones, gems, lab time, a run's worth of lost income, or an explicit statement that the move is free.
FAIL if there are more than three 'do now' moves, or any of them has no reason, or any of them says nothing at all about what it costs.
